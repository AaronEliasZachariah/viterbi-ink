Privacy policy

Your notes stay on your device.

Effective 17 July 2026

Viterbi Ink is a local-first note-taking and sketching app. The short version: your notes never leave your device, and we don't collect anything.

What we collect

Nothing. Viterbi Ink has no analytics, no advertising, no trackers, and no crash-reporting SDKs. The app does not transmit your notes, drawings, PDFs, images, recordings, or any usage data to us or to anyone else.

Where your data lives

Everything you create in Viterbi Ink — canvases, folders, imported PDFs and images, screen recordings, boards — is stored only on your device, in the app's private storage.

  • Exports (PNG, JPG, PDF, and backup files) are written only to locations you pick, or handed to apps you choose through the system share sheet.
  • Screen recordings are saved on your device. The microphone permission is requested only for recording and is optional — decline it and recordings are simply silent.
  • Uninstalling the app deletes its private data. Back up anything you want to keep first (Export → Backup file).

Cloud sync — optional, when available

A future version of Viterbi Ink will offer an optional account for syncing canvases between devices. If you choose to sign up, we will store your email address and the content you sync on our servers, solely to provide the sync service. Sync is off unless you create an account and turn it on — the app never uploads anything on its own. This policy will be updated with specifics (provider, retention, deletion) when the feature ships.

Purchases — when available

Paid upgrades are processed entirely by Google Play. We never see your payment details. Google's handling of that data is covered by the Google Play Terms of Service and Google's Privacy Policy.

The app reads only files you explicitly pick (PDFs and images to import) through the system file picker. It has no access to your broader storage, location, contacts, camera, or anything else.

Children

Viterbi Ink is a general-audience productivity tool. It is not directed at children under 13, and since it collects no data, it collects no data from children.

Changes to this policy

If a future version changes what data the app handles (for example, when cloud sync ships), this page will be updated and the effective date bumped before that version is released.
